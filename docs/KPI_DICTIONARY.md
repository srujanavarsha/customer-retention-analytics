# Enterprise KPI Dictionary & Business Taxonomy
**Business Unit:** Growth, Retention & Customer Analytics  
**Version:** 1.0 (Enterprise Production Standard)

| KPI Name | Business Definition | Technical Formula / Logic | Target Benchmark | Business Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cohort Retention Rate (M1)** | % of customers from a given acquisition cohort who place at least 1 completed order in Month 1. | `Active Customers (M1) / Total Cohort Size (M0)` | > 20.0% | Head of Retention |
| **RFM Champions** | High-value, loyal accounts with high recency, high purchase frequency, and high monetary spend. | `R Score >= 3, F Score >= 3, M Score >= 3` | Top 15% of Customer Base | VP Marketing / CRM |
| **At Risk (High-Value Churn)** | High historical spenders who haven't placed an order in over 180 days. | `R Score <= 2 AND (F Score >= 3 OR M Score >= 3)` | < 12% of Total Revenue Base | Retention Marketing |
| **Repurchase Cadence Anomaly** | Identification of a customer whose inactivity period exceeds 2.5x their historical average order interval. | `(Current Date - Last Order Date) > 2.5 * Avg Days Between Orders` | Real-time Alert Trigger | CRM Lifecycle Lead |
| **Negative Feedback Ratio** | Proportion of customer feedback reviews flagged with 1 or 2 stars and negative sentiment tags. | `Count(Negative Reviews) / Total Reviews Ingested` | < 10.0% | Head of Customer Experience |
| **Net Customer Lifetime Spend (LTV)** | Cumulative net revenue generated by an individual customer account over their lifespan. | `SUM(fact_orders[total_amount]) WHERE order_status = 'Completed'` | > $450 Average LTV | Chief Financial Officer |
